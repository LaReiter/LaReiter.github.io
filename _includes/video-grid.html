{%- comment -%} A 16:9 rectangle split into a grid of YouTube videos, one per cell. At rest every cell shows its video's thumbnail, dimmed. Hovering a cell (or tabbing to it, or a first tap on touch) expands it to fill the whole rectangle at full brightness with a play button, so it reads as an ordinary embed. Clicking then swaps in the real player, which stays until the back button below the rectangle is used. Usage, with the list in the page's front matter: videos: - id: hj8XWPTayS4 title: "Den russiske bondealgoritme" {% include video-grid.html videos=page.videos cols=2 %} The cells are facades: no iframe exists until a video is played, so the page does not load a YouTube player per video, and hovering back and forth reloads nothing.
