# Ghostwriter brief — personal website (lareiter.github.io)

Tone calibration for prose on this site. Layers on top of the base style guide.

## Dial position

Between *explaining to a general reader* and *personal reflection*. Warmer and less hedged
than the popular-science articles, because most of it is autobiographical fact rather than
argument. Never sales-y, never a LinkedIn summary.

## Register

- First person, plain, unhurried. Curiosity is the through-line: the site's organising idea
  is asking questions, not listing achievements.
- Concrete before abstract, in miniature: the About page opens on a children's book, not on
  "I am a statistician."
- Credentials are left to the CV page. Prose pages don't restate them.
- Self-deprecating parentheticals for scale ("a selected (tiny) fraction", "my (first)
  Python game"). Keep the parentheses in translation, they carry the tone.
- Short stacked declaratives for warmth: "I loved this book as a kid. I still love it today."

## Bilingual notes

The site runs English at `/` and Danish at `/da/`. Chrome strings live in `_data/ui.yml`,
prose lives in the page files.

- Danish is not a back-translation of the English. Where a reference is Danish in origin
  (Spørge Jørgen), the Danish version should read as the original and the English as the
  adaptation.
- Published article titles, paper titles and journal names are never translated. They are
  how the work is findable.
- Blog posts stay in whichever language they were written in. No obligation to mirror them.

## Hard constraints

No em dash. No semicolon. Applies in both languages. See style guide Section 6.
